# Speculative decoding at PP=4: prefill proven through middle ranks, decode still stuck

Box: 4× RTX 3090 (sm_86), driver 595.58.03, vLLM nightly `0.26.1rc1.dev649+g61874f984`,
torch 2.13.0+cu130. Model: Kimi-K3 4-layer slice (`--load-format dummy`) + DSpark
draft, `num_speculative_tokens=3`, `--enforce-eager`, `--pipeline-parallel-size 4`.

Backend patch under test is **upstream [vllm#51065](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/51065)**,
not our A6 look-alike (`SKIP_A6=1`). It applied to `dev649` with two hunk offsets.

## Why PP=4 and not PP=2

PP=2 has no middle rank: rank 0 only sends taps, rank 1 only receives. The
receive-and-forward branch (patches B1 + B2 in the same call) never executes.
At PP=4 ranks 1 and 2 do both. On the 47-card target that is 45 of 47 ranks, so
PP=2 evidence says almost nothing about the rig.

## Result

**Prefill through two middle ranks is exact.** The draft's tap fingerprint at
PP=4 equals the PP=1 reference to every printed digit, and equals the PP=2 value
measured earlier:

```
PP=1  shape=(11, 4096) sum=6.4611821672e-09 weighted=2.2653207910e-05 absmax=7.7307049651e-11
PP=4  shape=(11, 4096) sum=6.4611821672e-09 weighted=2.2653207910e-05 absmax=7.7307049651e-11
```

So the taps produced on ranks 0-2 are carried across two forwarding hops and
arrive at the draft on rank 3 bit-identical. That is the thing PP=2 could not
test, and it works.

**Decode does not complete.** After the prefill tap, the first decode step
deadlocks and the engine dies waiting on a worker:

```
EngineCore encountered a fatal error.
  File ".../multiproc_executor.py", line 416, in get_response
    status, result = mq.dequeue(timeout=dequeue_timeout)
```

At PP=1 the same request produces 24 decode taps and output text; at PP=4 it
produces one prefill tap and nothing further.

## Honest status of the goal

- Spec decode under PP is **proven end to end at PP=2** (boot, taps bit-identical, output).
- At PP=4 the **middle-rank tap path is proven**, decode is **not**.
- No speedup has been measured anywhere, and the draft carries dummy weights, so
  acceptance rate is meaningless. Do not put a throughput number on this yet.

## Two separate hangs, do not confuse them

1. **Warmup** (worked around by `SKIP_KERNEL_WARMUP=1`, used for the run above).
   Diagnosed by `faulthandler` stacks: three ranks sit in `warmup_kernels ->
   sample_tokens -> postprocess_num_computed_tokens` while the last rank has
   already moved on to `warmup_kernels -> _run_decode_step -> execute_model`.
   The ranks are in different phases of the warmup script, so the sampled-token
   broadcast has receivers and no sender; NCCL's watchdog fires at 600s on
   `WorkNCCL(SeqNum=1, OpType=BROADCAST, NumelIn=1024)` — 1024 = 256 requests ×
   (1 + 3 spec tokens), i.e. exactly `pp_handler.receive`'s `sampled_tokens`.

   Before that, a *different* warmup failure hid this one: FlashInfer JIT-builds
   its sampling module on first use, on the last rank only, and the image has no
   `nvcc`/`ninja`. `VLLM_USE_FLASHINFER_SAMPLER=0` removes it.

2. **Decode at PP=4** (open). Same shape — engine waiting on a worker — but it
   happens with warmup skipped, on a real request.
