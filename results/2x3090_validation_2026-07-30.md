## Capacity anchor (hardware-validated)

One full-size K3 MoE layer in packed MXFP4 = **15.72 GB → fits a 3090 with
9.3 GB spare; two layers OOM.** vLLM PP splits by whole layers ⇒ on 24 GB
cards full K3 is **~1 MoE layer/card ≈ 93 cards**, PP-only. The ~70-card
"byte floor" in `CAPACITY_50x3090.md` is reachable only with intra-layer
sharding (TP/EP), which this rig excludes. 50 cards cannot serve full K3
under any vLLM quantization that exists today.
