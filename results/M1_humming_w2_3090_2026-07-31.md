# M1 gate: Humming W2A16 on RTX 3090 — PASSED (2026-07-31)

Setup: vast 2×3090 box, wheel `vllm-0.26.1rc2+sm86` + **PR #48918 applied as
python overlay** (2 hunks hand-ported — our tree derives layout choice from
`is_transposed`, subsuming the PR's `uses_checkpoint_layout` hunks).
Instrument: `prepare_humming_layer` on a `ReplicatedLinear` at the K3
LatentMoE w13 shape (N=6144, K=3584), CT pack-quantized int, group 128,
`HummingMethod.forward_layer`, 200 timed iters after warmup.

## Result: W2 == W4 in time, half the bytes

| m | W4 µs | W2 µs | W2/W4 (pass ≤1.3) | W4 eff GB/s |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 48.0 | 41.9 | **0.87** | 237 |
| 4 | 41.9 | 42.1 | 1.01 | 273 |
| 16 | 41.5 | 42.1 | **1.01** | 281 |
| 64 | 54.9 | 55.0 | 1.00 | 229 |
| 256 | 193.0 | 190.5 | 0.99 | 85 |
| 2048 | 1355 | 1338 | 0.99 | (compute-bound: ~67 of ~71 TFLOPS bf16 — 94% peak) |

- **Gate criterion (W2 ≤ 1.3× W4): PASSED at every m** — the 2-layers-per-card
  capacity win costs nothing in GEMM time vs W4.
- Absolute small-m efficiency ≈ 25–30% of the 936 GB/s peak — launch/
  dequant-overhead-bound, consistent with the A800 (~28%) datapoint from the
  scoping research. Same overhead-bound regime as every other kernel we've
  measured on sm_86; CUDA graphs and batching will matter more than bits.
- Humming ships a dedicated `Sm86Heuristics` class — sm_86 is a first-class
  target for the library.

## Residual risks (NOT covered by M1)

1. This probes the **dense** humming path; the grouped-MoE experts path
   (`HummingExpertsBase`, PR #48918) may have different overheads. Real check
   = serving a W2-quantized slice (next step after PR lands or via overlay).
2. Checkpoint production: **GSQ at 2.8T scale is unpriced** — nobody has
   published a W2 GSQ of K3's experts. This is now the biggest unknown on
   route (b).
3. Quality of W2 experts — same open question as M0; the two gates converge:
   if UD-Q2_K_XL (a *smarter* dynamic 2-bit) disappoints on M0, GSQ-W2
   (uniform) will not do better.
